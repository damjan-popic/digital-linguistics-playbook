My research connects language, its social use and the infrastructure through which we describe it. Data is a starting point, not an answer in itself.

## Corpus linguistics and language in society

I use corpora to investigate usage, differences between texts and linguistic variation. I am particularly interested in social context, language contact and expectations about what counts as “correct” language.

## Translation and language technology

My work includes translation workflows, terminology, natural language processing and the evaluation of language tools. With AI, I am less interested in a convincing performance than in whether we can check an answer and understand its limitations.

## Slovene in Italy

A substantial part of my work concerns language resources, terminology support and tools for the Slovene community in Italy. This is a setting where the difference between an interesting prototype and a useful service really matters.

## Digital humanities and heritage

I work with digital collections, connections between texts and metadata, and the documentation of cultural heritage, including in Ladakh. The tools should serve the research question, not replace it.
