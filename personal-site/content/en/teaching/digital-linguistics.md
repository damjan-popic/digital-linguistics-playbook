A starting point for practical work with language data. The focus is not on tools for their own sake, but on the questions they can help us answer.

## Where to start

The [Digital Linguistics Playbook](https://damjan-popic.github.io/digital-linguistics-playbook/) brings together practical workflows and explanations. Start with the problem you want to solve, rather than a list of software.

## Areas of work

- Corpora: text selection, querying, concordances and interpretation.
- Terminology and translation workflows: from identifying terms to organising resources.
- Language technology: text processing, evaluation and reproducibility.

## Before asking the tool

What is your research question? Which data could help address it? What would count as a good answer — and how would you recognise a bad one?

Choosing the tool is easier once those three answers are at least roughly clear.
