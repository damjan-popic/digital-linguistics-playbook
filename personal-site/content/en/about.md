My background is in translation, with a PhD in Translation Studies. My work centres on corpus linguistics, digital humanities and language technology. I enjoy research questions that need both linguistic judgement and a little technical ingenuity.

## Where I work

My work connects the University of Ljubljana and the Slovene Research Institute in Trieste. These are the official institutional profiles:

- [University of Ljubljana, Faculty of Arts — Department of Translation Studies](https://www.ff.uni-lj.si/zaposleni/damjan-popic).
- [University of Ljubljana, Faculty of Computer and Information Science — Laboratory for Machine Learning and Language Technologies](https://fri.uni-lj.si/sl/o-fakulteti/osebje/damjan-popic).
- [Slovene Research Institute — SLORI](https://www.slori.org/dr-damjan-popic/).

Information about my teaching, including English at AGRFT, is collected under [Teaching](teaching/index.md).

## What interests me

How to draw defensible conclusions from language data. How to build useful tools for smaller language communities. And how to connect questions from the humanities with methods that other people can inspect, repeat and improve.

When teaching, I try to explain not just which button to press, but what pressing it actually does.
