## Materials

Start with [Teaching](teaching/index.md) and choose your course or area. The [English · AGRFT](teaching/agrft.md) page is for the winter-semester course in 2026/27.

## How to contact me

Email [damjan.popic@ff.uni-lj.si](mailto:damjan.popic@ff.uni-lj.si). Include the course in the subject line and enough context in the message for me to understand the question.

For a longer conversation, we can arrange a time by email. It is better to ask when you get stuck than to spend three weeks silently staring at the same error.

## When a tool refuses to cooperate

Explain what you wanted to do, what you tried and what happened. For a technical problem, the exact error message and a small example help. Remove passwords, access keys and personal information from screenshots first.

## Assignments, grades and privacy

Follow your course instructions for submitting work. This site is public, so it is not a place for submitted assignments, grades, student lists or access-restricted materials.

The use of language and generative tools also depends on the instructions for the particular assignment. When those instructions are unclear, ask.
