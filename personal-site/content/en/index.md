## A little less official.

I'm a linguist, researcher and teacher. I'm interested in language as people use it, the data we use to describe it, and tools that help us do something useful with both. Sometimes that means corpus analysis. Sometimes programming. Quite often, both.

This site brings together my research, projects and teaching. For formal details, see my [institutional profiles](about.md). For materials, practical information and useful links, start with [the student pages](students.md).
