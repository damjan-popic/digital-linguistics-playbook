A selection of projects and tools I work on. Not an exhaustive catalogue — just a few useful places to start exploring.

## KLIKai

Connecting language resources with answers to practical language questions. The goal is an answer whose supporting sources can also be checked, rather than a piece of text that merely sounds plausible.

[Open KLIKai](https://klikai.jeziknaklik.it/) — a pilot service; access may be restricted.

## CROSSTERM

Terminology across borders: Slovene, Italian and Friulian. The project connects terminology work with tools for finding and recognising specialised terms.

[Terminology portal](https://crossterm.slori.org/) · [Official project description](https://www.slori.org/projekti/crossterm-crossborder-standardizationof-institutional-terminology/)

## Digital Linguistics Playbook

An open collection of practical workflows, questions and answers for working with language data. Useful for learning, teaching and the moment when a real problem refuses to cooperate.

[Open the Playbook](https://damjan-popic.github.io/digital-linguistics-playbook/)

## Digital heritage in Ladakh

Connecting texts, metadata and research resources to document and investigate cultural heritage. I am particularly interested in building collections that can be meaningfully explored, rather than simply stored.

[Related research projects in SICRIS](https://cris.cobiss.net/ecris/si/sl/researcher/38181)
